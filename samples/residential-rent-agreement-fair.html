Leave and Licence Agreement

Executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka · On this 1st day of October 2026 · On stamp paper of Rs. 200/-

BETWEEN Mrs. Latha Venkataraman, aged 51 years, residing at No. 22, 9th Main, Indiranagar, Bengaluru 560038 (hereinafter the "Licensor")

AND Ms. Priya Menon, aged 27 years, presently employed at Bengaluru (hereinafter the "Licensee").

1. Licensed Premises and Term

The Licensor grants to the Licensee leave and licence to occupy Flat No. 1104, Green Meadows, 12th Main, Koramangala 5th Block, Bengaluru 560095, admeasuring 1,080 sq. ft., for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1st October 2026 and expiring 31st August 2027.

2. Licence Fee and Escalation

The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 40,000/- (Rupees Forty Thousand only) on or before the 7th day of each calendar month. Upon renewal, any increase in the licence fee shall be mutually agreed between the parties in writing, and shall not in any event exceed five percent (5%) over the fee then payable.

3. Security Deposit

The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 80,000/- (Rupees Eighty Thousand only), equivalent to two (2) months licence fee. The said deposit shall be refunded within thirty (30) days of the Licensee vacating and handing over possession. Any deduction shall be supported by an itemised written statement together with receipts or estimates, and no deduction shall be made on account of normal wear and tear. Any dispute regarding a deduction shall be referred to a mutually appointed mediator before any other remedy is pursued.

4. Lock-in Period

This Agreement shall be subject to a lock-in period of two (2) months from the date of commencement, applicable equally to both parties. Should either party terminate before expiry of the lock-in period, that party shall pay to the other a sum equivalent to one (1) month licence fee, and no further amount shall be claimed.

5. Notice to Vacate

Either party may terminate this Agreement after the lock-in period by giving one (1) month prior written notice. Where the Licensor alleges a breach by the Licensee, the Licensor shall give written particulars of the breach and a period of fifteen (15) days to remedy it before terminating on that ground.

6. Maintenance and Repairs

All structural repairs, and all repairs to plumbing, electrical wiring, seepage and waterproofing, shall be carried out by the Licensor at the Licensor's own cost within a reasonable time of being notified. The Licensee shall be responsible only for minor day-to-day maintenance and for damage caused by the Licensee's own negligence.

7. Painting Charges

Repainting at the end of the term shall be charged at actuals against a written estimate, and only where repainting is necessitated by damage beyond normal wear and tear. Where the Licensee has occupied the premises for the full term without such damage, no repainting charge shall be levied.

8. Entry and Inspection

The Licensor or her authorised representative may enter the licensed premises for inspection upon giving the Licensee not less than twenty-four (24) hours prior notice, and such entry shall be at a time convenient to the Licensee. In case of a genuine emergency affecting safety or the structure, entry may be made without notice, and the Licensor shall inform the Licensee as soon as practicable thereafter.

9. Restrictions on Use

The licensed premises shall be used for residential purposes by the Licensee and immediate family. The Licensee may keep one domestic pet upon informing the Licensor in writing. The Licensee may work from the premises, provided such work does not involve regular visits by clients or members of the public. The Licensee shall not sub-let or part with possession without the prior written consent of the Licensor, which shall not be unreasonably withheld.

10. Society Charges and Utilities

Monthly maintenance charges levied by the co-operative housing society shall be borne by the Licensor. Electricity, water, gas, internet and any other utility consumed by the Licensee shall be borne by the Licensee as per actual meter readings.

11. Stamp Duty and Registration

The cost of stamp duty and registration of this Agreement shall be borne equally by both parties, and the parties shall attend before the Sub-Registrar for registration within thirty (30) days of execution.

12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall first be referred to mediation by a mediator appointed by mutual consent. Failing resolution within forty-five (45) days, the dispute shall be subject to the jurisdiction of the competent courts at Bengaluru. The costs of mediation shall be shared equally.
