Leave and Licence Agreement

Executed at Bengaluru, Karnataka · On this 1st day of October 2026 · On stamp paper of Rs. 200/-

BETWEEN Mr. Rajendra Prasad Iyer, aged 58 years, residing at No. 14, 3rd Cross, Jayanagar 4th Block, Bengaluru 560011 (hereinafter the "Licensor")

AND Ms. Priya Menon, aged 27 years, presently employed at Bengaluru (hereinafter the "Licensee").

1. Licensed Premises and Term

The Licensor grants to the Licensee leave and licence to occupy Flat No. 302, Sunrise Residency, 7th Main, HSR Layout Sector 2, Bengaluru 560102, admeasuring 1,150 sq. ft., for a period of eleven (11) months commencing 1st October 2026 and expiring 31st August 2027.

2. Licence Fee and Escalation

The Licensee shall pay a monthly licence fee of Rs. 42,000/- (Rupees Forty Two Thousand only) on or before the 5th day of each calendar month. Upon renewal, the licence fee shall stand increased by ten percent (10%) over the fee then payable, and such increase shall not be subject to negotiation.

3. Security Deposit

The Licensee has paid an interest-free refundable security deposit of Rs. 4,20,000/- (Rupees Four Lakh Twenty Thousand only), equivalent to ten (10) months licence fee. The said deposit shall be refunded within ninety (90) days of the Licensee vacating and handing over peaceful possession, after deduction of all amounts the Licensor may in his sole discretion determine to be owing on account of damage, repainting, deep cleaning, or unpaid dues. The determination of the Licensor in this regard shall be final and binding.

4. Lock-in Period

Notwithstanding anything contained herein, this Agreement shall be subject to a lock-in period of six (6) months. Should the Licensee vacate the premises before expiry of the lock-in period for any reason whatsoever, the entire licence fee for the unexpired portion of the lock-in period shall immediately become due and payable, and the Licensor shall be entitled to adjust the same against the security deposit.

5. Notice to Vacate

Either party may terminate this Agreement after the lock-in period by giving two (2) months prior written notice. The Licensor may, however, terminate this Agreement with fifteen (15) days notice in the event of any breach by the Licensee, the existence of such breach to be determined by the Licensor.

6. Maintenance and Repairs

All repairs of whatsoever nature, whether structural or otherwise, including but not limited to plumbing, electrical fittings, seepage, waterproofing and civil works, shall be carried out by the Licensee at the Licensee's own cost. The Licensor shall bear no liability for the condition of the premises.

7. Painting Charges

The Licensee shall pay a sum equivalent to one (1) month licence fee towards repainting charges at the time of vacating, irrespective of the actual condition of the premises and irrespective of the duration of occupancy.

8. Entry and Inspection

The Licensor or his authorised representative shall be entitled to enter the licensed premises at any reasonable hour for the purpose of inspection, and the Licensee shall not unreasonably withhold access. In case of emergency, no notice shall be required.

9. Restrictions on Use

The licensed premises shall be used solely for residential purposes by the Licensee and immediate family. The Licensee shall not keep pets, shall not sub-let or part with possession, and shall not carry on any commercial activity, including work-from-home activity involving visitors, without prior written consent of the Licensor.

10. Society Charges and Utilities

Monthly maintenance charges payable to the apartment owners association, presently Rs. 4,500/- per month, shall be borne by the Licensee. Any increase in such charges during the term shall also be borne by the Licensee. Electricity and water charges shall be paid by the Licensee as per actuals.

11. Stamp Duty and Registration

The cost of stamp duty and registration of this Agreement shall be borne entirely by the Licensee.

12. Dispute Resolution

Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator to be appointed by the Licensor, and the seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru. The courts at Bengaluru alone shall have jurisdiction.
