Independent Contractor Services Agreement

This Agreement is made on 20th September 2026 between Hexafold Design Studio LLP, having its registered office at 402, Aurum Towers, Andheri East, Mumbai 400069 ("the Client"), and Ms. Kavya Raghunathan, an independent contractor residing at Kochi, Kerala ("the Contractor").

1. Scope of Services

The Contractor shall design and deliver a complete brand identity system including logo, typography, colour system, stationery and a brand usage manual, together with such other deliverables as the Client may reasonably require in connection therewith.

2. Fees and Payment Schedule

The total fee for the Services is Rs. 1,80,000/- payable as follows: twenty percent (20%) on signing, thirty percent (30%) on presentation of initial concepts, and fifty percent (50%) on final acceptance of all deliverables by the Client. Final acceptance shall be at the sole satisfaction of the Client.

3. Revisions

The Contractor shall provide revisions to the deliverables until the Client is satisfied. No additional fee shall be payable in respect of any revision, regardless of the number of revision rounds or the extent of change requested.

4. Payment Timelines

Invoices raised by the Contractor shall be settled within sixty (60) days of receipt. No interest shall be payable on delayed payments. The Client may withhold any payment in the event of any dispute regarding the deliverables, until such dispute is resolved to the Client's satisfaction.

5. Intellectual Property

All intellectual property in the deliverables, including all preliminary concepts, sketches, rejected options and working files, shall vest in the Client upon creation. The Contractor shall not use, display, or reference any of the work, including in a personal portfolio or on social media, without the prior written consent of the Client.

6. Exclusivity

During the term of this Agreement, the Contractor shall not provide services of a similar nature to any business engaged in the same industry segment as the Client or any of the Client's customers.

7. Termination

The Client may terminate this Agreement at any time, with or without cause, by written notice with immediate effect. In such event, the Client shall pay only for milestones fully completed and accepted as at the date of termination, and no payment shall be due in respect of work in progress. The Contractor may terminate only upon thirty (30) days written notice and upon completion of all work then in progress.

8. Indemnity

The Contractor shall indemnify and hold harmless the Client, its partners, employees and customers against all claims, damages, costs and expenses of any nature arising out of or in connection with the deliverables, without any cap on liability.

9. Independent Contractor Status

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed to create an employment relationship. The Contractor shall be solely responsible for her own income tax, goods and services tax, and all statutory contributions. The Client shall deduct tax at source as required under applicable law.

10. Confidentiality and Survival

The Contractor shall keep confidential all information relating to the Client's business. Clauses 5, 6, 8 and 10 shall survive termination of this Agreement.

11. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India. The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction.
